If you've searched for root canal prices in Pune, you've probably seen numbers ranging from suspiciously cheap to alarmingly high. Both extremes usually leave something out. Rather than quote a number that may not apply to your tooth, here is what actually decides the cost — so you can read any quote intelligently.

## The four things that decide the price

**Which tooth it is.** Front teeth usually have one canal; premolars have one or two; molars have three or four. More canals means more work, more time and a higher fee. A molar root canal costs meaningfully more than a front tooth almost everywhere.

**How complex the canals are.** Curved, narrow or calcified canals take longer and need more care. This is often only fully visible on the X-ray, which is why an honest clinic examines first and quotes after.

**Whether it's a first treatment or a retreatment.** Redoing a failed root canal is harder than doing one on an untreated tooth, and is priced accordingly.

**The crown.** A root-canal-treated tooth usually needs a crown to protect it from fracturing. Some quotes include it; many don't. Always ask whether the number you've been given is *with or without the crown* — this is the single biggest source of surprise bills.

## Why the cheapest quote is often the most expensive

A root canal that fails because canals were missed or poorly sealed has to be redone — or the tooth extracted and replaced with an implant, which costs several times more than the root canal would have. Paying a fair price once is cheaper than paying a low price twice.

## What you should expect from any clinic

- An examination and X-ray *before* a final quote
- A written estimate that clearly says what's included — treatment, medication, crown
- An explanation of why your tooth needs (or doesn't need) the treatment
